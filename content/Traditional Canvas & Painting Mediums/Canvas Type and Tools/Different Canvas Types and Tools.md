Stretched cotton and linen canvases offer distinct flexible surfaces. While wood panels provide rigid support ideal for heavy paint. Preparing surfaces with gesso creates proper adhesion and prevents degradation. Artists manipulate paint using flat, round, or filbert brushes alongside palette knives for thick application. Smooth palettes hold and mix colors, while water or solvents clean brushes and thin mediums. Choosing the right surface tooth and tool stiffness dictates brushwork texture, control, and overall durability.
