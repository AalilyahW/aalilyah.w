# Canvas Types and Surfaces

Choosing the right support surface is critical to the longevity and texture of a painting. The surface texture, known as the tooth, determines how paint grips the substrate and moves under the brush.

## Cotton Duck and Linen:

Artists primarily choose between two natural woven textiles:

* **Cotton Duck Canvas:** Economical and widely available, cotton duck is ideal for students and professionals alike. It stretches easily and works well for small to medium-sized paintings.
* **Belgian Linen:** Linen features long flax fibers, making it exceptionally strong and resistant to warping or sagging over time. Its tight, irregular weave provides a luxurious surface preferred for archival works.

### Canvas Formats

1. **Stretched Canvas:** Fabric stretched tightly over wooden stretcher bars, offering a springy, traditional feel.
2. **Canvas Panels:** Fabric mounted onto rigid MDF boards, perfect for outdoor plein air painting.

> "A well-primed canvas with proper surface tension provides the foundation for every successful brushstroke."
