# Acrylic Techniques

Acrylic paint is one of the most versatile and durable mediums available to modern artists. Formulated with pigment suspended in an acrylic polymer emulsion, it dries rapidly and becomes water resistant when cured. Artists like me can manipulate acrylics to mimic the delicate washes of watercolor or the heavy impasto textures of oil paint.

## Application Methods

Mastering acrylics involves experimenting with super speed and layering techniques:

1. **Dry Brushing:** Applying minimal paint on a dry brush to create textured, opaque strokes over underlying layers.
2. **Glazing:** Mixing thin layers of transparent acrylic medium to build up complex, glowing color shifts.
3. **Scumbling:** Scrubbing a light layer of opaque paint thinly over a darker dry area to produce a soft, atmospheric effect.

### Wet on Wet vs. Layering

Because acrylics dry quickly, working wet on wet requires speed or retarding mediums. But, rapid drying makes acrylics ideal for fast layering without disturbing underlying colors.
