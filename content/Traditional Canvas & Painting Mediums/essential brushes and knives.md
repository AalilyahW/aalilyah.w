# Essential Brushes and Knives

The interaction between paint and canvas relies heavily on the tools used to apply it. Selecting the appropriate combination of synthetic or natural brushes and palette knives gives artists complete control over texture and edge quality.

## Brush Shapes and Hair Types

Different brush shapes fulfill distinct roles in composition:

* **Flats & Brights:** Square-edged brushes useful for wide coverage, sharp lines, and bold rectangular strokes.
* **Filberts:** Oval-shaped brushes that blend smooth edges and soft transitions, combining features of flats and rounds.
* **Rounds:** Pointed brushes designed for detailed linework, precision highlights, and lettering.

Brush hair types vary: stiff hog bristles handle thick oil paint effortlessly, while soft synthetic fibers excel with liquid acrylics.

### Painting Knives for Texture

Flexible metal palette knives allow artists to apply unmixed paint in sculptural, heavy impasto layers. Knives create clean edges and flat color fields that brushes cannot replicate.
