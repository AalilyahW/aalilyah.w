### Brush Engine Customization 

* Fine-tuning digital brush dynamics (grain, opacity jitter, wet mix simulation) to replicate traditional mediums.

### Imitating Physical Canvas

* Using background texture overlays, canvas grain brushes, and impasto light effects to mimic physical paint thickness and substrate tooth.

--------------
#### Breakdown:
1. One of the most interesting things about digital art software is how it can recreate the look and feel of traditional art on a flat screen. Different brush settings allow artists to adjust things like texture, opacity, blending, and the shape of their strokes to make digital brushes look more like oil paint, watercolor, or charcoal. 

2. Some software can even imitate how paint spreads and blends depending on how much pressure or speed is used with the stylus. Artists can add canvas textures, grain, and other effects to make their work look more realistic and give it more depth. 

3. Traditional painting still has the advantage of actually feeling the texture of the canvas and the thickness of the paint, but digital tools can come surprisingly close visually. At the same time, artists still have the flexibility to edit, undo, and make changes without affecting the original artwork.
